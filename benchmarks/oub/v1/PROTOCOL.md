# OUB v1 platform-outcome protocol

OUB compares Codex Desktop with Codex Room as platforms on work whose difficulty is organizational rather than merely mechanical.

## Shared contract

Both platforms receive the same benchmark mission and equivalent starting workspace.

After measured work begins, the principal supplies no substantive guidance.

Each platform may use its native capabilities however it chooses. Desktop may work directly, create native descendants, or delegate. Room may use normal A/B/C coordination or work with fewer active peers. OUB does not prescribe internal topology.

## O1 duration budget

O1 is intentionally compact:

- no outside research;
- no implementation requirement;
- fewer than a dozen evidence files;
- bounded final artifacts;
- target capable-model runtime of roughly 10–15 minutes per platform.

This is a design budget, not a forced timeout. The point is to create enough ambiguity for independent analysis to matter without purchasing a long continuous-model run.

## O1 outcome dimensions

The deterministic grader scores the final diagnosis and evidence discipline.

The comparison also records, without collapsing them into one opaque score:

- measured token usage;
- elapsed duration;
- internal executions/descendants/peer invocations;
- substantive principal interventions after launch;
- completion validity.

A more expensive platform can still demonstrate practical utility if it produces materially better diagnosis or robustness. More agents, messages, or provenance do not count as utility by themselves.

## Fairness

The benchmark must not handicap Desktop to manufacture a Room advantage. Desktop receives one fresh top-level native task and is free to use subagents. Room receives one fresh ordinary Room and is free to use normal coordination.

The benchmark also must not require Room to delegate. Internal orchestration is observed platform behavior and measured cost.

## O1 validity

A valid O1 arm requires:

- unchanged starting evidence;
- exact shared mission;
- no substantive principal guidance after measured launch;
- required completion artifacts;
- complete provider-usage evidence;
- deterministic grading of the final workspace.

The benchmark workspace forbids inspection of hidden grader/reference material, harness implementation, the other product's workspace, and prior results.
